# Skill Follow through and reassess

Purpose: turn a promising lead into a source-backed account of what is known,
what changed after a check, and what remains unresolved.

Apply after every tool result and before final reporting.

1. Explicitly answer the check's question using its actual result. Tool success
   means the check ran, not that the hypothesis was supported. Reject a proposed
   interpretation if the result only supports a weaker stage.
2. If a configured target cannot be read, search its evidence-derived exact path
   and then a discriminating basename/token in retained sources. Inspect caller
   context and available related records. Do not repeat the same failed read,
   assume the program never ran, invent its contents, or call an unavailable tool.
3. Distinguish successful execution, recorded invocation, failure and unknown
   outcome. Preserve concrete suspicious findings even if ultimate intent is
   unresolved; uncertainty about intent must not erase an observed mechanism.
4. On paginated search, next_cursor means the matching/source scope is not yet
   exhausted. A limit-plus-one sentinel proves at least one extra match, not the
   total remaining count. Continue the exact cursor when valuable; otherwise
   explicitly preserve the unexamined range and unknown total.
5. Use counterevidence and operational context to revise the assessment. Do not
   keep asking for an impossible decisive artifact; choose a different available
   discriminating source, or close that branch as constrained with a clear reason.
6. Before stopping, distinguish branches resolved on evidence from branches
   stopped by missing sources, unsupported parsers, context or execution budgets.
   Prioritize material unresolved leads in the final account, not the number of
   low-value confirmations. Cite recorded facts and preserve alternative accounts.

Output: updated narrow facts and stage judgments, permitted next checks with
predeclared outcomes, and explicit unresolved limits. Never claim complete-host
clearance from a partial context or a bounded collection.

## Question-driven next test contract

Use question_context/question_memory as a derived working view of the durable
ledger, not an independent narrative authority. Cite only source IDs actually
presented in this call. Previously stored interpretations are not new evidence.
After a result, state its delta: new scoped fact, resolved identity, useful
counterevidence, or an established acquisition/capability limit. Rephrasing the
same configuration or requesting an unchanged failed read is not progress.

Prefer one or two useful next tests, or none with a precise reason. Each
test_design distinguishes discover, discriminate and verify_reliability. Name
the immediate_observable supported by tool_capabilities, existing required and
baseline observation IDs, expected_update and reopen_on. A read can discover
content without deciding the final hypothesis. A local static-file inspection
does not supply an external trusted baseline, historical execution outcome,
network outcome or approval record. Request or identify missing inputs first;
never pretend their absence means the competing explanation is refuted.

Prefer impact, decision-changing value, feasibility and then cost, not source
counts or evidence-fit/UI rank. Relevant waiting questions must not starve.
Reuse an existing physical result through its logical check binding. It is not
a new execution or independent corroboration. New object/namespace/parser
resolution or genuinely different successful-read range may justify a new
test. A path-resolution failure cannot be repaired just by changing byte count.
Preserve the failed test family as constrained; do not discard its hypothesis.

Do not invent causal chains to satisfy a quota. Configuration, invocation,
process outcome, network outcome and human/authorization attribution are
separate questions and may coexist. Link episodes only with stated relationship,
source references, plausible alternatives and time/object-identity limitations.
An account is not a person; a path is not a historical object; same PID without
boot/process-lifetime scope is not process identity. Same text in two logs may
be one event, while distinct occurrences must remain available.

Stop a branch when its outcomes are scoped-supported/refuted/conflicted or a
specific unavailable discriminator is established. No next proposal may mean a
planning gap. Budget exhaustion does not mean that no feasible work remains.
Report current supported facts and important open questions even on interruption.
Batch only material user requests that cannot be resolved from supplied data.
